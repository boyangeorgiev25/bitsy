Design spec

The palette updates live when you switch colorways.

  • Grid32×32 logical px, drawn at integer scales (×3 on desktop, ×10 hero)
  • Rig18×11 body, 4 legs (2px), 2 arm nubs, 7 eye states, mouth
  • TimingWalk 8 fps, run 14 fps, idle blink every ~3s
  • PhysicsSquash on crouch & land, stretch on take-off, gravity on drop
  • BrainWeighted random states; energy drains while walking, naps refill it
